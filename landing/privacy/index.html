Privacy Policy

Effective 3 August 2026

ChefPocket is a meal-planning app for university students. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we do not run advertising or tracking, and we never sell your data.

What We Collect

  • Account details. Your name, email address, and password when you create an account. Passwords are stored in hashed form and we never see them in plain text.
  • Profile details. Optional information you add, such as your gender and your school. This helps us set your daily nutrition targets and place you on your school's leaderboard.
  • Preferences and goals. Your dietary preferences, cooking equipment, and goal (bulk, cut, or maintain), collected during onboarding.
  • Plans and lists. The meal plans, recipe queues, and shopping lists the app generates for you.
  • Academic schedule. Class and exam times you enter or import so we can schedule meals around them. We never change your calendar.
  • Cooking activity. Meals you mark as cooked, your streak, and your achievements.
  • Purchase records. If you subscribe in the future, purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.

What We Do Not Collect

ChefPocket contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising.

How We Use Your Data

  • To generate meal plans and shopping lists that match your preferences, schedule, and budget.
  • To track your cooking streak and show your rank within your school's community. Leaderboards show your display name only.
  • To keep you signed in and keep your data in sync across sessions.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.

Where Your Data Lives

Your data is stored with Supabase, which provides our authentication and database infrastructure. Recipe photography is loaded from Unsplash. Future subscriptions would be processed by Apple and managed by RevenueCat. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

How Long We Keep It

Your data is kept while your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is deleted from our systems.

Deleting Your Account

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@chefpocket.app. An in-app deletion option is coming with our App Store release.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Email us and we will help. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Children

ChefPocket is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@chefpocket.app.
